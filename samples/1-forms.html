1-forms: form controls and validation

Compiled from sass/1-forms.sass into css/1-forms.css, which this page loads.

Form placeholders style controls, labels, help text, selects, checks, switches, input groups, floating labels, range, file and colour inputs, and validation. The full reference is in docs/1-forms.md.

Your full name, as it appears on your passport.
@use "sassy/src" as sassy

    <label for="name">Name</label>

</div>

Select chevron in a dark theme

Selects draw their chevron as an image, so a dark theme sets --sassy-select-chevron with chevron() as well as the text colour.

@use "sassy/src" as sassy

@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)
    :root
        --sassy-input-colour: #{sassy.colour("grey", 100)}
        --sassy-select-chevron: #{sassy.chevron(sassy.colour("grey", 100))}
