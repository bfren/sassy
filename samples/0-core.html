0-core: loading and configuring Sassy

Compiled from sass/0-core.sass into css/0-core.css, which this page loads.

The foundation of Sassy: the settings, the make mixins that generate placeholders, and helper mixins and functions. Load it once per site with @use "sassy/src" as sassy, and configure it with with (...). The full reference is in docs/0-core.md.

Palette colours and transparency

A tooltip-style label, with a transparent background from grey 900
@use "sassy/src" as sassy

.tooltip
    background-color: sassy.with-alpha(sassy.colour("grey", 900), 0.9)
    color: sassy.colour("grey", 50)

Your own placeholders, in Sassy's style

A stack of one or two items, with a larger gap from md. The menu extends %bf-stack-1 and %bf-stack-2-md.

One Two 
@use "sassy/src" as sassy

@include sassy.make-passes
    @include sassy.make-responsive("stack", (1: 0.25rem, 2: 0.5rem)) using ($value)
        display: flex
        flex-direction: column
        gap: $value

.menu
    @extend %bf-stack-1
    @extend %bf-stack-2-md

.menu-named
    @extend %#{sassy.make-name("stack", 2)}
