Text, layout, colour, display, border, corners and shadow

The utilities layer, src/4-utilities, has the single-purpose placeholders: each sets one property, or one family of properties, to one value. The full reference is in docs/4-utilities.md.

Each demo on this page is styled by sass/4-utilities.sass, compiled to css/4-utilities.css. Under each demo are its Sass, the CSS that Sassy outputs for it, and its HTML.

Coloured shadows

Shadows take their colour from --sassy-shadow-colour, which is black at low opacity by default. Set it on an element, or a section, to colour its shadows - here to the theme's primary colour.

The default shadow
A shadow in the primary colour
.shadows
    @extend %bf-d-grid
    @extend %bf-gap-4

.panel-primary
    --sassy-shadow-colour: var(--sassy-theme-primary)
<div class="shadows">
    <div class="panel">The default shadow</div>
    <div class="panel panel-primary">A shadow in the primary colour</div>
</div>
