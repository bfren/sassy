3-grid: rows and columns

Compiled from sass/3-grid.sass into css/3-grid.css, which this page loads.

A 12-column flex grid. A row extends %bf-d-flex-row, and each column extends %bf-col-{1-12}, with a breakpoint variant for each width you want to change. The full reference is in docs/3-grid.md.

Two equal columns that stack on small screens

Resize the window: the columns sit side by side from md upwards.

Right
@use "sassy/src" as sassy

.row

    @extend %bf-col-6-md
<div class="row">
    <div class="half">Left</div>
    <div class="half">Right</div>
</div>
